Physical and Chemical Changes

Learn about physical changes, chemical changes, rusting and crystallisation.


📺 Lesson 1

In this lesson, we will learn how physical and chemical changes differ and explore examples such as rusting.


📚 Notes

A physical change generally changes the physical properties of a substance without forming a new substance.

A chemical change results in the formation of one or more new substances with different properties.

Rusting is a chemical change in which iron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form rust.
