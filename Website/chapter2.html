Nutrition in Animals

Learn how animals obtain food, digest it and absorb nutrients.


📺 Lesson 1

In this lesson, we will learn about the different steps of nutrition in animals, the human digestive system and digestion.


📚 Notes

Animals cannot prepare their own food like green plants. They obtain food from plants or other animals.

The main steps of nutrition are ingestion, digestion, absorption, assimilation and egestion.
