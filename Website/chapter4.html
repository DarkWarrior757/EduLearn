Acids, Bases and Salts

Learn about acids, bases, indicators and neutralisation.


📺 Lesson 1

In this lesson, we will learn about acids and bases, natural indicators, neutralisation and salts.


📚 Notes

Acids are substances that generally have a sour taste, while bases generally have a bitter taste and feel soapy.

Indicators are substances that help us identify whether a solution is acidic or basic.

When an acid reacts with a base, a neutralisation reaction occurs and salt and water are generally formed.
