Heat

Learn about heat, temperature, thermometers and transfer of heat.


📺 Lesson 1

In this lesson, we will learn about heat and temperature, different types of thermometers, and how heat moves from one object to another.


📚 Notes

Heat is a form of energy that flows from a hotter object to a colder object.

Temperature tells us how hot or cold an object is. Temperature is commonly measured using a thermometer.
